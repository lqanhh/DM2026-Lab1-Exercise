Next, I moved on to the filtering steps. I personally used a 0.01 threshold because **variance_filter_11** which ran with a 0.001 threshold did not manage to remove anything. Spearman correlation filtering was then applied to the *-1.0* label using a threshold of 0.05 (ID **spearman_filter_13**). Out of 4,313 original terms, 362 were retained and 3,951 were removed. I did the same thing with *0.0* (ID **spearman_filter_14**; kept 438 - removed 3,878), and *1.0* (ID **spearson_filter_15**; kept 696 - removed 3,617).
